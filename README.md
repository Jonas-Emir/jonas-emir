## About Me & What I Do

Building resilient corporate systems and solving complex real-world challenges through modern software architecture. My approach to engineering combines technical depth with the high-discipline and strategic mindset forged during my Reserve Officer Training and throughout my professional journey.

**Here is what I can implement and bring to your team:**

- **System Modernization & Architecture:** Expertise in migrating legacy monolithic systems (.NET Framework, Windows Forms) to modern, scalable architectures using ASP.NET Core and Angular. Strong grasp of microservices, event-driven systems (Kafka/RabbitMQ), and containerization (Docker).
- **Performance & Stability:** Deep experience in Level 3 Support, refactoring legacy codebases, and optimizing SQL scripts to resolve severe bottlenecks and maintain high availability in production environments.
- **Leadership & Agile Mentorship:** Certified in Scrum Foundation. I value collective growth and have hands-on experience mentoring junior developers, conducting technical onboarding, and writing documentation for the long-term maintenance of the systems.
- **AI & IoT Enthusiast:** Currently deepening my knowledge in AI Engineering (data orchestration and LLMs integration). In my free time, I extend my logic skills to hardware, building home automation and robotics using Arduino and ESP32.

*Based in Brazil | Open to global opportunities*
